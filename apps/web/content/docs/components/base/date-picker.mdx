Click the field and pick a day from the calendar. With `hasTime`, a time wheel sits beside the calendar, as in the iOS Clock app: the field reads `Nov 12, 2026, 10:30 AM`, and the value is that day at that time. With `granularity="month"` or `"year"`, a grid of twelve months or years takes the calendar's place. For people who'd rather type, `editable` adds a text field that reads `2026-11-12`, `Nov 12, 2026` or `tomorrow at 9pm`.

The `DatePicker` field is part of the Fabricator style. It's composed from [Popover](/docs/components/base/popover), [Calendar](/docs/components/base/calendar) and the [Time Input](/docs/components/base/time-input) wheel, with Fluid Hover on the month and year grid, and framed like every other field, so the focus ring and invalid state match. With `editable`, the field is an [Input Group](/docs/components/base/input-group).

## Usage

```tsx

```

```tsx
<DatePicker aria-label="Due date" />
<DatePicker aria-label="Starts" hasTime />
<DatePicker aria-label="Billing month" granularity="month" />
```

## Composition

```text
DatePicker
├── PopoverTrigger               the field: the date, or its placeholder
├── PopoverContent
│   ├── Calendar                 one month, single selection
│   ├── TimeWheel                with `hasTime`: hours, minutes, seconds, AM or PM
│   └── grid                     instead of both, at a month or year granularity:
│                                twelve cells, three to a row, under the page's arrows
└── input[type=hidden]           with `name`: yyyy-MM-dd, yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm(:ss), yyyy-MM or yyyy
```

With `editable`, the field is an `InputGroup` instead: an `InputGroupInput` for the text and an `InputGroupButton` that opens the same popover.

`className` goes on the root, for layout. `id`, `placeholder`, `disabled` and the `aria-*` labels go to the field; with `editable`, every other input prop goes to the text input as well.

## Examples

### Field

Put it in a `Field` with a `FieldLabel` pointing at its `id`, and a `FieldDescription` for what to pick.

<ComponentPreview styleName="base-nova" name="date-picker-field" />

### Date and time

`hasTime` puts a [Time Input](/docs/components/base/time-input) wheel beside the calendar, as tall as it: hours, minutes, AM or PM on a 12-hour clock, and seconds with `hasSeconds`. It opens on the value's time, or now. Pick a day and turn the wheel: the field shows both and the value follows as each column settles. A new day keeps the time. Enter, Escape or a click elsewhere closes the popover. `step` sets the minutes between the minute wheel's rows (1 by default), and the clock follows the reader's locale unless `hourCycle` fixes one.

<ComponentPreview styleName="base-nova" name="date-picker-date-time" />

### Month and year

`granularity="month"` picks a month from a year of them; `granularity="year"` picks a year from a page of twelve. The arrows beside the heading turn the page, one highlight glides between the cells, and a new page slides in from the side it came from. The value is still a `Date`: the first day of the month or year. The text reads `Mar 2028` or `2028`; `format` changes it. `minDate` and `maxDate` compare whole months or years, and `hasTime` doesn't apply.

With `editable`, a month reads `Mar 2028`, `March 2028`, `2028-03`, `3/2028` and `this month`, `next month` or `last month`; a year reads `2028` and `this year`, `next year` or `last year`. Use a month for a card's expiry or a billing period, where the browser would offer `<input type="month">`.

### Typing

`editable` makes the field a text input with a calendar button, for people who know the date already. The text is read on Enter or when the field loses focus, then rewritten in the display format. With `hasTime`, the time comes after a comma or "at": `Nov 12, 2026, 10:30 AM` or `tomorrow at 9pm`. See [What it reads](#what-it-reads).

<ComponentPreview styleName="base-nova" name="date-picker-editable" />

### Controlled

Control the value with `value` and `onValueChange`. It changes when a day (and, with `hasTime`, a time) is picked, or when typed text is committed, not on every keystroke.

<ComponentPreview styleName="base-nova" name="date-picker-controlled" />

### Format

`format` is a [date-fns pattern](https://date-fns.org/docs/format) for the text, `MMM d, yyyy` by default. With `editable`, typing reads the same pattern, and ISO `yyyy-MM-dd` always works. Pass a date-fns `locale` to write and read month names in another language; it also sets the calendar's.

<ComponentPreview styleName="base-nova" name="date-picker-format" />

### Minimum and maximum

`minDate` and `maxDate` turn off the days outside them in the calendar, and stop its navigation at their months. With `editable`, a typed date outside them is rewritten, marked invalid, and leaves the value empty.

<ComponentPreview styleName="base-nova" name="date-picker-min-max" />

Pass `aria-invalid` to show your own validation, with the message in a `FieldError`. With `editable`, text that isn't a date sets it on its own.

### In a form

With `name`, a hidden input sends the day as `yyyy-MM-dd`, or `yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm` with `hasTime` (`yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm:ss` with `hasSeconds`), whatever the display format. A month sends `yyyy-MM`, what `<input type="month">` submits, and a year `yyyy`. With `editable`, Enter commits the text first; a second Enter submits.

## Compositions

`DatePicker` is one date, with or without a time. For a range, presets, or a range with times, compose a [Calendar](/docs/components/base/calendar) in a [Popover](/docs/components/base/popover) yourself. There is no root component for these; each example is the whole recipe.

```text
Popover
├── PopoverTrigger
└── PopoverContent
    └── Calendar
```

For times on a range, put a `TimeRangeInput` from [Time Input](/docs/components/base/time-input) under the trigger: a start and an end with the duration between them. See [Date and Time Range](#date-and-time-range).

## What it reads

With `editable`, the text field reads:

| Typed                                                                                            | Becomes                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| The display format (`Nov 12, 2026`)                                                              | That day                                |
| ISO (`2026-11-12`)                                                                               | That day                                |
| `today`, `tomorrow`, `yesterday`                                                                 | That day, from the person's clock       |
| With `hasTime`, a time after a comma or `at` (`Nov 12, 2026, 10:30 AM`, `tomorrow at 9pm`)       | That day at that time                   |
| A month: `Mar 2028`, `March 2028`, `2028-03`, `3/2028`, `this month`, `next month`, `last month` | The first of that month                 |
| A year: `2028`, `this year`, `next year`, `last year`                                            | The first of that year                  |
| Nothing                                                                                          | No value                                |
| Anything else, or a day that can't be                                                            | No value; the text stays and is invalid |

Case and extra spaces don't matter. Years need four digits, so `Nov 12, 26` isn't read as the year 26. The rules are pure functions in `lib/date-picker-model.ts` (`parseDateText`, `commitDateText`, `formatDateValue`, `formatIsoDate`, `formatIsoDateTime`, `setDateTime`, `splitDateTimeText`, `isDateInRange`, and `getDateGridOptions` for the grid), each taking a `granularity`, which you can use for your own validation.

## Accessibility

- The field is a button that reports `aria-expanded`. Label it with a `FieldLabel` pointing at its `id`, or `aria-label`; the placeholder isn't a label.
- Enter, Space or a click opens the calendar on the selected day's month, with focus on that day. Arrow keys move by day and week, Page Up and Page Down by month, Enter or Space picks, and Escape closes.
- With `hasTime`, Tab moves from the calendar to the wheel, a group named by `timeLabel`. Each column is a `spinbutton` (Hours, Minutes, Seconds, AM or PM): Arrow Up and Down step it, Page Up and Page Down jump five, Home and End go to the ends, and Tab moves to the next column. Enter closes the popover.
- At a month or year granularity, the cells are a grid with focus on the selected cell, or this month or year. Left and Right move by one, Up and Down by three, Page Up and Page Down by a page of twelve, Enter or Space picks, and Escape closes. The heading is announced as it changes.
- Without `hasTime`, picking a day, month or year closes the popover. Closing returns focus to the field.
- With `editable`, the text input is a plain text field and Alt+ArrowDown opens the calendar (announced through `aria-keyshortcuts`); its button is named by `calendarLabel`. Text that isn't a date sets `aria-invalid`.
- `aria-invalid` turns the frame red, and screen readers announce it.
- Disabled, the field leaves the tab order and the hidden input isn't submitted.
