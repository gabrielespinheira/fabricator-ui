## Installation

```bash
npx fabricator-ui@latest add record-view
```

## Usage

```tsx
<RecordView
  open={open}
  onOpenChange={setOpen}
  mode={mode}
  onModeChange={setMode}
  context="Accounts"
  title={account.name}
  onTitleChange={rename}
  cover={<RecordViewCover src={account.cover} alt="" />}
  aside={<PropertyList properties={properties} getValue={getValue} />}
>
  <RecordViewSection title="Notes">{notes}</RecordViewSection>
</RecordView>
```

## Modes

- **Side:** a panel over a screen that stays live. It isn't modal, so opening another card swaps what is inside.
- **Center:** a modal dialog.
- **Page:** the whole window. With room for two columns, the `aside` moves beside the content.

On phones every mode takes the screen. The app owns which record is open and its URL.

Switching between the side panel and the other modes draws the content again, because React Aria’s overlays are always modal. Save a draft as it is typed.

## Cover

`cover` takes a `RecordViewCover`, a picture across the top. Its children are controls over the picture, such as a "Change" button. A picture that doesn't load leaves no gap.

## States

`state` takes `ready`, `loading`, `missing` or `error`, with `onRetry` for the last. `isReadOnly` marks the record and stops its title being edited.

## Accessibility

The surface is a dialog named by its title. Opening the side panel from the keyboard moves focus into it; a pointer leaves focus on the card, so arrows keep moving along the board. Escape closes it.
