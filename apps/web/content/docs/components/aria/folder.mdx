Hover a folder, or tab to its link: the files rise and fan out, and the front panel tips forward. Moving away, they drop back in on a quicker timing. Each folder takes any palette colour, solid or glass, and shows up to three files: blank sheets, thumbnails or anything you draw.

Folder is part of the Fabricator style. It's built on HTML, so the same component ships for every base.

## Composition

```text
Folder                    data-slot="folder" data-variant data-size [data-open]
├── FolderPreview         data-slot="folder-preview"
│   ├── span              data-slot="folder-back"
│   ├── span              data-slot="folder-lining" (behind glass)
│   ├── FolderFiles       data-slot="folder-files" data-count
│   │   └── FolderFile    data-slot="folder-file" data-index
│   │       └── span      data-slot="folder-file-lines" (a blank sheet)
│   ├── span              data-slot="folder-front"
│   └── FolderCover       data-slot="folder-cover"
├── FolderTitle           data-slot="folder-title"
└── FolderDescription     data-slot="folder-description"
```

- **FolderFiles** shows its first three children; the first lies on top. Leave it empty, or out, for an empty folder.
- **FolderFile** is a blank sheet with lines until you give it children: an `img` fills it, and anything else lays out inside it. It's sized in shares of the folder's width, so `cqw` units keep text in step at any size. Colour text on a sheet with `text-(--folder-paper-ink)`: sheets stay light in dark themes.
- **FolderCover** rides on the front panel: badges, avatars, icons. Badges turn frosted pills of your brightest surface and avatars are ringed in the front's colour.
- **FolderTitle** holding a link or button makes the whole tile its target: it takes the click, the focus ring and the open pose on focus.

## Examples

### Colors

`color` takes a palette colour (`blue`, `teal`, `orange`, …), a role (`primary`, `success`, …) or any CSS colour. The folder is drawn from your surfaces, tinted with a share of that colour the way a Kanban column or a property chip is, so it sits in the page instead of shouting over it, and follows a surface tint and both themes. Text on the cover takes the colour's text token. Unset, the tint is your muted text colour.

<ComponentPreview styleName="aria-nova" name="folder-colors" />

### Glass

`variant="glass"` frosts the front panel: the files show through it, blurred, clearer at the top and denser towards its foot. Open, the pane tips forward less than a solid front and slides a little down, so the lifted files stay behind clear glass.

<ComponentPreview styleName="aria-nova" name="folder-glass" />

### Previews

Put thumbnails, type or colour in a `FolderFile`, or leave it empty for a blank sheet when there's no preview to show.

<ComponentPreview styleName="aria-nova" name="folder-previews" />

### Empty and file counts

A folder shows up to three files, however many you pass. With none it's an empty folder: hover still tips the front open, onto nothing.

<ComponentPreview styleName="aria-nova" name="folder-empty" />

### Sizes

`sm`, `default` and `lg` tiles are 120, 180 and 240px wide, and pin the compact, default and spacious density for the text below them. Put `w-full` on the tile to fill a grid cell instead.

<ComponentPreview styleName="aria-nova" name="folder-sizes" />

### Grid

A drive's grid: `w-full` tiles in an `auto-fill` grid, each with a link in its title. A long name truncates; pass it as the title's `title` to show it whole on hover.

<ComponentPreview styleName="aria-nova" name="folder-grid" />

### Drop target

`open` holds the open pose without hover: here while a file is dragged over the folder, and for the folder whose contents are on view.

<ComponentPreview styleName="aria-nova" name="folder-drop-target" />

## Motion

Opening, the files rise one after another on the bounce spring, fanned from their foot like a hand of cards, while the front tips forward on the move timing. Closing, everything drops back together on the quicker exit timings, without the bounce. A press gives the tile to 97%. Reduce Motion or motion off keeps the poses and drops the travel.

## API Reference

### Folder

| Prop      | Type                        | Default     |
| --------- | --------------------------- | ----------- |
| `color`   | `string`                    | (neutral)   |
| `variant` | `"default" \| "glass"`      | `"default"` |
| `size`    | `"sm" \| "default" \| "lg"` | `"default"` |
| `open`    | `boolean`                   | `false`     |

The root is a `div` and takes its props and `ref`. Every other part is a `div` that takes its props.

## Accessibility

The drawing is decorative: the files are hidden from screen readers, and the folder is named by its title. Put a link or a button in `FolderTitle` and the whole tile becomes that one control, named by the title, reached with Tab and opened with Enter. Anything on the cover stays readable, so give an icon an `aria-label`. The open pose follows focus as it follows hover, so the keyboard sees the same preview, and touch, with no hover, opens on the link.
