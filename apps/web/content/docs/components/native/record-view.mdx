## Installation

```bash
npx fabricator-ui@latest add record-view
```

## Usage

```tsx
<RecordView
  open={open}
  onOpenChange={setOpen}
  mode={mode}
  onModeChange={setMode}
  context="Accounts"
  title={account.name}
  onTitleChange={rename}
  cover={<RecordViewCover src={account.cover} alt="" />}
  aside={<PropertyList properties={properties} getValue={getValue} />}
>
  <RecordViewSection title="Notes">{notes}</RecordViewSection>
</RecordView>
```

## Modes

- **Side:** a drawer on a phone, a panel along the edge on a tablet.
- **Center:** a drawer on a phone, a centred sheet on a tablet.
- **Page:** the whole screen. On a wide screen the `aside` sits beside the content.

Compose a screen in the app's navigator for a record with its own route.

## Cover

`cover` takes a `RecordViewCover`, a picture across the top. Its children are controls over the picture, such as a "Change" button. A picture that doesn't load leaves no gap.

## States

`state` takes `ready`, `loading`, `missing` or `error`, with `onRetry` for the last. `isReadOnly` marks the record and stops its title being edited.

## Accessibility

The record is named by its title. The header's controls have names, and the title takes focus only when it is pressed.
