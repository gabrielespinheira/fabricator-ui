---
title: "EventCalendarHeader and Navigation"
description: "Arrange the title, date controls, view picker, settings, and creation button."
---

`EventCalendarHeader` lays out the controls. `EventCalendarTitle` shows the active period and opens a date picker. `EventCalendarNavigation` moves by the current view's step, `EventCalendarToday` returns to today, and `EventCalendarViewSelect` switches views. `EventCalendarSettings` exposes options such as weekends, week numbers, and clock format.

```tsx
<EventCalendarHeader>
  <EventCalendarTitle />
  <EventCalendarToday />
  <EventCalendarNavigation />
  <EventCalendarViewSelect />
  <EventCalendarSettings />
  <EventCalendarCreateButton />
</EventCalendarHeader>
```

Use a subset for a smaller header. Keep `EventCalendarCreateButton` inside the calendar or pass it the same controller when it sits in a separate toolbar. A view can also be controlled from your application through `view` and `onViewChange`.
