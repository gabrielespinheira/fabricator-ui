---
title: "EditorKeyboardBar and Word Count"
description: "Add editing commands above the keyboard and feedback below the document."
---

`EditorKeyboardBar` exposes formatting above the on-screen keyboard. `EditorFooter` lays out supporting information, and `EditorWordCount` reads the editor document to display its word count.

```tsx
<Editor defaultValue="A short draft">
  <EditorContent aria-label="Draft" />
  <EditorKeyboardBar />
  <EditorFooter>
    <EditorWordCount />
  </EditorFooter>
</Editor>
```

Use the keyboard bar with editable documents. Leave the word count out of a large-document workflow if the information has no role there. It reads the document text to compute the count.
