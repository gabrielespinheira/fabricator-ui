---
title: November 2026 - Easier checkbox and radio rows
description: Checkbox and radio labels take clicks across the whole row, with more room between rows and between a control and its label.
date: 2026-11-07
---

- **The whole row toggles.** A label placed after a checkbox or radio now takes clicks 8px above and below its text, so a click between two rows still lands on one. Next to a description, the title and the description both toggle the control. Links in a description stay clickable. Rows keep their height.
- **More room.** Radio groups and checkbox groups space their rows 16px apart instead of 12, and a horizontal Field leaves 10px between a checkbox or radio and its label instead of 6. Both follow the density. On the web and on React Native.
