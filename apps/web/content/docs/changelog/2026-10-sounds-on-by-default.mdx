---
title: October 2026 - Sounds on by default
description: Interface sounds now play as soon as <SoundEffects /> mounts.
date: 2026-10-11
---

`@fabricator/sounds` now starts with sounds on. Mount `<SoundEffects />` and every component plays its sound right away, with no extra setup. To let people turn sounds off, pass their preference as `<SoundEffects enabled={soundsOn} />` or call `setSoundsEnabled(false)`. The docs site plays sounds by default too, and the Sound setting turns them off.

See [Sounds](/docs/foundations/sounds).
