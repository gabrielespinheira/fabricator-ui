---
title: November 2026 - Shorter component group names
description: Six single-word component groups across the docs, desktop and mobile examples.
date: 2026-11-10
---

The component groups are now Basics, Forms, Overlays, Structure, Content and Chat across the documentation, desktop example and mobile example.

Overlays replaces Overlays & Menus, Structure replaces Navigation & Layout, Content replaces Data & Feedback, and Chat replaces AI & Chat. Components keep their existing groups, order and links. The groups also keep their navigation IDs so saved preferences still apply.
