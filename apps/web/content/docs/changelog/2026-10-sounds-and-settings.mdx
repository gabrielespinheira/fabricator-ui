## Sounds

`@fabricator/sounds` adds interface sounds, synthesized with the Web Audio API: no audio files and no dependencies. There are 15 short voices for taps, selection, checks, switches, popups, dialogs, sliders, refusals, notifications and completion. The catalogue is inspired by [Bencho's sound library](https://bencho.dev/sounds); the sounds themselves are our own.

Mount `<SoundEffects />` once at the root of your app and every component plays the right sound, in every base, with no changes to the components themselves. Sounds are off until you turn them on. Sounds and the pill radius also install in existing projects that keep their own style, because they only rely on attributes every component shares. Use `data-sound` to change or silence a sound, and `playSound` or `useSound` for your own interactions. See [Sounds](/docs/foundations/sounds).

```bash
npx fabricator-ui@latest add sounds
```

## Pill radius

Every corner comes from the `--radius` token. `@fabricator/radius-pill` sets it to `1.25rem`, so controls become full pills and containers stay concentric with their items. See [Sizes](/docs/foundations/sizes#rounded-or-pill).

```bash
npx fabricator-ui@latest add radius-pill
```

## Site settings

The header, the docs sidebar and the docs side panel have a settings menu:

- **Theme:** light or dark.
- **Sound:** turns the component sounds on or off.
- **Icons:** Lucide (the default), Tabler, HugeIcons, Phosphor or Remix Icon, applied live to every preview.
- **Radius:** Rounded or Pill.

## Cursors

Every Fabricator component now shows the cursor that matches what a press does:

- `pointer` on anything that activates: buttons, toggles, tabs, accordion triggers, checkboxes, radios, switches, select and combobox triggers, and every menu, select, combobox and command row. Labels of checkboxes, switches and radios get it too.
- `text` on input-OTP slots and the combobox chips row.
- `grab`, then `grabbing`, on slider thumbs and drawer handles; the slider track is `pointer`.
- `not-allowed` on disabled controls and slider thumbs, and on buttons that stay focusable while disabled.

The rules live in the style, so `className` still overrides them.

## Search

A new component: a round search button that springs open into the field as one object, takes focus, and closes again on Escape or when you leave it empty. It has `default` and `outline` variants, three sizes, a clear button, and controlled or uncontrolled `open` and `value`. See [Search](/docs/components/base/search).

```bash
npx fabricator-ui@latest add search
```

## Checkbox and Radio Group

Both are 18px with a quiet outline. Checked, they fill with the foreground colour, and an inverted check or dot pops in on the bouncy spring. They give a little when pressed, and focus is the 1px ring with an offset.

## Smoother rendering

- The foundations turn on grayscale font anti-aliasing, legibility and kerning, optical sizes, and no faux bold or italic, for every app that uses Fabricator components.
- Dialogs and alert dialogs are centered without a half-pixel `translate`, so their text stays sharp at every window size.

## Removed

- **Native Select** is no longer part of the Fabricator library: it only restyled the browser's own `<select>`. Use [Select](/docs/components/base/select). Blocks and examples that used it now use Select. Projects that keep their own style can still install it.

## Fixes

- Calendar and Date Picker: hovering the selected day no longer hides its number, and keyboard focus uses the 1px focus ring.

- Sidebar rows that open a collapsible now get the Fluid Hover highlight in every base.
