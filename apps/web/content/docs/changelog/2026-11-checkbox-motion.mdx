---
title: Checkbox motion
description: The checkmark draws itself in, and indeterminate shows a minus.
date: 2026-11-12
---

Checking a Checkbox now draws its mark in from the short leg while it fades and grows from 0.6. Unchecking shrinks it back through a small blur and fades it out, quicker than it came in. Indeterminate draws a minus instead of a check, and moving between checked and indeterminate retracts one stroke while the other draws. Every timing follows the motion speed, and reduced motion turns the transitions off.

The mark is drawn inline on every base, so it no longer comes from the project's icon library. React Native draws the same strokes with `react-native-svg` on the same timings, without the blur.
