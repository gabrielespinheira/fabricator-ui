---
title: Avatar Group tooltips and parting
description: Optional avatar tooltips, a springy group hover and colour demos.
date: 2026-11-14
---

`Avatar` takes an optional `tooltip`, a string or the tooltip content's props, that names the person on hover and keyboard focus. An avatar with a tooltip is focusable and announced as an image named by it. In an `AvatarGroup`, moving along the avatars swaps names without replaying the tooltip's entrance.

The group now parts around the avatar under the pointer or keyboard focus: it lifts 2px, grows to 1.06 and comes forward, while the avatars before it slide back and those after it slide on, on the bounce spring. It runs for a fine pointer only, and reduced motion keeps the avatar coming forward without the moves. On React Native the same parting follows a held avatar, and a long press shows its tooltip.

Avatars in demos and blocks are no longer drawn in grayscale.

Update with `npx fabricator-ui@latest add avatar`. It now installs `tooltip` too.
