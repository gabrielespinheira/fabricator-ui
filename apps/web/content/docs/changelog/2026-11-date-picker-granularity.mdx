---
title: Date Picker picks months and years
description: Month Input is now Date Picker with granularity="month", and granularity="year" picks a year.
date: 2026-11-12
---

Date Picker takes a `granularity`: `"day"` (the default), `"month"` or `"year"`. A month or a year opens a grid of twelve instead of the calendar, with the gliding highlight, the sliding selection and a page that slides in from the side it came from. It works as a button field or, with `editable`, as a text field that reads `Mar 2028`, `3/2028`, `2028-03` and `next month`, or `2028` and `next year`. The value is a `Date` on the first day of the month or year, and a form gets `yyyy-MM` or `yyyy`. On the web and on React Native.

**Breaking:** Month Input is gone. Replace `<MonthInput />` with `<DatePicker granularity="month" editable />`. Its `"yyyy-MM"` string values become `Date`s: `value="2028-03"` is `value={new Date(2028, 2, 1)}`, and `minMonth` and `maxMonth` are `minDate` and `maxDate`. `pickerLabel` is `calendarLabel`. A form still receives `yyyy-MM`. Run `npx fabricator-ui@latest add date-picker` and delete `components/ui/month-input.tsx` and `lib/month-input-model.ts`.
