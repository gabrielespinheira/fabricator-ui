---
title: October 2026 - One style
description: Every component now ships in the Fabricator style only. Blend mode is removed.
date: 2026-10-11
---

The registry now serves one style: the Fabricator design shown in these docs. Blend mode, which compiled components in your project's own shadcn style, is removed.

- **One registry URL:** `https://fabricator-ui.vercel.app/r/fabricator/{style}/{name}.json`. The base is read from your `style` field, and colours, radius and fonts come from your theme.
- **Old URLs keep working:** `https://fabricator-ui.vercel.app/r/{style}/{name}.json` redirects to the Fabricator style. Run `npx fabricator-ui@latest doctor` to find it, then update the `@fabricator` entry in `components.json`.
- **CLI:** `init` and `apply` no longer accept `--blend`. "Add the @fabricator registry only" writes the new URL.

See [Registry](/docs/registry).
